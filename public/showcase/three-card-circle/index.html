THE ON-CHAIN RECORD

Every move leaves a trace.

Each transfer updates one shared ownership record in the same transaction. Eligibility comes from the owners before that transaction. The chain keeps the history.

Transfers stay inside programmable addresses. There is no return-only pool, no public mint queue and no extra editions. The proposed launch issues all three to the creator; burning is disabled.

Technical evidence & activation status

645 contract checks pass, including 27 tests of the new circle rules. Thirteen signed emulator transactions succeed; ten invalid attempts are rejected. A mainnet node evaluates an allowed transfer successfully and rejects a forbidden one, using synthetic inputs. The test mint is 15,194 bytes against a 16,384-byte limit. These checks are not an independent audit or a mainnet deployment.

Measured transfer fees range from about 0.57 to 0.70 ADA. The initial three-card mint costs about 1.02 ADA and is included in the activation total. Fees are not refunded.

CIP-113 is a proposed Cardano standard. This collection uses its programmable-token model, with a custom ownership module.
