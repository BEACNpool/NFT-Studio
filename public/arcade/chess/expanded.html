How to play

Play White, Black or both sides. Tap a piece, then a highlighted square. Choose queen, rook, bishop or knight when a pawn promotes. Castling and en passant follow the referee's legal moves.

The computer uses fixed-depth search: Gentle is depth 1; Deeper is depth 2. It may pause briefly while thinking. Hint is a suggestion, not a guaranteed win. Undo restores your previous turn against the computer.

This edition automatically ends on checkmate, stalemate, threefold repetition, the 50-move rule, or the referee's conservative insufficient-material test. It has no clock or tournament claim procedure. Save codes replay legal moves from the initial position.

Engine: Ledger Chess 1.0.0, MIT licensed. The original referee source is preserved unchanged; this edition supplies a new compact interface.
