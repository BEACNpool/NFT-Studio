You are not alone. You are earlier.

Move with arrows, WASD, the pad or an adjacent tile. The center dot / Space waits one turn. Reach the diamond exit.

Stand on a numbered rune, then Make echo (R). You return to the start. Your amber memory replays those exact moves as you move, then holds its last tile. You can record up to three echoes.

A gate opens when its rune is occupied on that turn. Echoes are fixed memories: later doors cannot rewrite them. Leave a memory on each switch, or record a timed handoff between switches.

Undo (Z) takes back your last turn and moves the echoes back with it. Erase echo removes the newest memory and restarts your current route. Restart clears the room.

Listen turns your routes into a repeating musical canon without changing the puzzle. The timeline lets you inspect any recorded turn. Sound starts only when you enable it.

Build a room and share it as a memory capsule. A custom room may be impossible; test your creation. This program is public and reusable. Play happens on your device.
