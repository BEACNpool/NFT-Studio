a demonstration, for the Tipsy Turtles team

Three of your frames,
with nothing underneath them.

Your snippets are PNGs pinned on IPFS. What is on Cardano is a pointer — and a pointer is only as permanent as whoever keeps paying to pin it. Each of these is the same frame redrawn as vector art and written into the minting transaction itself. No gateway, no pin, no bill.

Together that is 4.1 MB of pinned PNG on one side and 35.2 kB of transaction on the other. Open any of them: the left panel is fetched live from your gateway, the right is decoded from the chain.

They are not equally faithful, and that is the point

A user interface comes back essentially exact — its pixel art is not even a recreation, it is the same grid of cells, extracted and re-encoded. A calm bust is a faithful redraw. A face pulled into a shout is the hardest of the three, because the expression lives in exactly the small shapes a byte budget wants to delete. Held beside the originals at full size the differences are visible, and they are stated on each page rather than glossed.

What none of them need is a gateway, a pin, or anyone still paying a bill in ten years.
