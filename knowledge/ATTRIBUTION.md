# Attribution and reuse

The Cardano standards summarized here are maintained in the
[Cardano Foundation CIPs repository](https://github.com/cardano-foundation/CIPs).
The catalog identifies the individual proposal authors, declared license, pinned
commit, raw source and SHA-256 for every standard. Most reviewed CIPs declare
CC-BY-4.0; CIP-27 and CIP-143 declare Apache-2.0. Consult each linked source before
reusing its text, code, examples or normative artifacts.

Our summaries, criticism, lifecycle analyses and proposed compositions are
original BEACN Labs writing. We have not republished the full specification
corpus, personal operational documents or third-party artworks. Linked upstream
projects retain their own licenses. The catalog's source author names identify
attribution; inclusion is not endorsement by those authors.

Tool sources include Aiken, EMURGO Cardano Serialization Lib, Lucid Evolution,
Mesh, Cardano Solutions Ogmios/Kupo, Koios, Hydra, Yaci DevKit and Mithril. See the
catalog for exact repository snapshots and upstream contributors. A dated
release observation is not a license determination or a security recommendation.

Use the repository's license terms for BEACN material where applicable. Do not
infer rights to mint third-party art, music or trademarks from the availability
