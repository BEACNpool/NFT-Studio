The picture below is the token: every byte of it lives in the minting transaction. No IPFS, no server, nothing to keep alive.

Three delegates take the stage. Two of them are him, at an angle, in glass. That is what the smoke is for — not to hide the man, to stop you checking how many there are.

He holds the only keyring because there was only ever one set of keys. For the first 4,490,510 blocks — every block from 2017-09-23 to 2020-07-29 — the chain names its own producers, and there are seven of them, all called ByronGenesisKey, taking turns within 3,000 blocks of each other. Nobody else could produce a block at all. Shelley kept the same seven as its genesis delegates and set the quorum at five, and 1,215,383,026 ada then left the reserves and the treasury on signatures alone, years before any holder could vote on anything.

None of that was hidden. It was published, in the genesis file, in plain JSON, the whole time. "Decentralized." The sign was always the trick — you were never going to walk up and count.
