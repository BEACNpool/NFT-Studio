Recall a song / how this instrument works

Four melodic orbits have cycles of 5, 7, 11 and 13 steps. Tap a phase button to rotate that orbit against the others. All notes follow the changing harmony. The complete clock-phase pattern repeats after 5,005 half-beat steps; each export is a sixteen-bar composition through that larger pattern.

Codes recall musical choices, tempo and edits. Nothing is saved automatically. WAV exports the full stereo performance; MIDI exports the notes for your own instruments. MIDI timbres will differ. Sound starts only after you press Play. Settings stop playback; press Play to hear your changes.

The composition rules, instruments and visuals are embedded in this NFT. Audio samples are generated on your device. No external samples or network requests. Publicly playable, without wallet access. Browser audio rendering can vary slightly. The orbital clock period describes rhythm phase, not 5,005 different songs. Sound, harmony and seed choices create additional variations.
