02 / REBUILD

Every frequency is a circle.

Magnitude sets radius; angle sets phase. Positive k turns counterclockwise; negative k clockwise. k = 0 is the center.

Read the error

RMS distance / RMS radius at 128 samples. Full band recovers samples, not in-between detail. Nyquist occurs once at −64; circle order cannot change the sum.
