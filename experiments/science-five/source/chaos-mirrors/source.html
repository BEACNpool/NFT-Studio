Inside the experiment

Ideal rods: massless, rigid, 1 m. Point masses: 1 kg. Gravity: 9.81 m/s². Angles measured from down. Only B's lower initial angle changes. No friction, collisions or random forces.

RK4 integration, Δt = 1/480 s. Small-angle motion is nearly regular; large-angle motion can be chaotic. Reset repeats the initial state [θ₁, θ₂, ω₁, ω₂].

E = ω₁² + ω₂²/2 + ω₁ω₂ cos(θ₁−θ₂) + g(3−2cos θ₁−cos θ₂)

Error: larger |E−E₀|/E₀ × 100%, using each pendulum's own E₀. This is numerical drift. Long-term paths are approximate; no Lyapunov exponent is measured.

Graph: lower-tip distance, not phase space; zero clips to 10⁻⁸ m. History: 60 s maximum. Hidden pages pause; slow devices may lag. Reload resets controls.

Equations: Erik Neumann, myPhysicsLab (2023). This does not forecast Cardano.
