Open the field notes — what does random mean?

Each result starts with a browser-generated 32-bit value. Rejection sampling discards the small incomplete range before taking a remainder, avoiding modulo bias. This is local entertainment, not a price prediction, a chain oracle or a lottery service.

Independent: every answer has probability 1/24, including the last one. Fresh: the previous answer has probability zero; each other answer has probability 1/23. Deck: choose uniformly from remaining cards; repeats become possible when a new deck begins. Changing the rule resets the experiment.

Shannon entropy H = −Σ p log₂(p) measures uncertainty in bits. The next-draw value is log₂(eligible answers). The empirical value describes the accumulated histogram; small samples fluctuate and can underestimate the population entropy. A flat histogram does not prove that a source is secure.
