    Learn · hashes and proofs

    Bytes → fingerprint

    SHA-256 gives the same 256-bit digest for the same bytes. It is not encryption; short messages can be guessed. Educational code, not a security library.

    UTF-8, no normalization. Bit 0 is the first byte’s high bit. Flipped bytes may be invalid text.

    Separate the domains

    Leaf: H(00 || text). Branch: H(01 || left || right). One-byte prefixes; raw 32-byte digests. Leaf order matters.

    Tree construction: RFC 9162 §2.1; not its full protocol.

    Choose your root

    Proofs show a position under a chosen root, not truth, identity, ownership or blockchain inclusion.

    SHA-256 demo. Cardano primarily uses Blake2b for ledger hashing. No wallet, network or storage.
