VELVET SOLITAIRE

Fifty-two cards. One way home.

A little patience. A lot of possibility.

Draw one. Build down. Bring every suit home.

How to play

Build tableau columns downward in alternating colors. Move a face-up sequence by tapping its first card, then its destination. Only kings can fill an empty column. Newly exposed tableau cards turn face up automatically.

Build the four suit foundations from ace to king. Tap a selected single card again to send it to a legal foundation, or tap the foundation explicitly. Foundation cards may return to the tableau. Tap the stock to draw one card; recycle the waste when the stock is empty.

Choose Open Book for a solvable practice deal: free the four aces in the right-hand columns, then build foundations. Seeded deals are shuffled normally and are not guaranteed solvable. The same seed reproduces the same deal.

Hints suggest a move; they do not solve the whole deal. Undo freely and keep a portable save to resume. The game has no wagering, prizes, wallet access or blockchain moves.
