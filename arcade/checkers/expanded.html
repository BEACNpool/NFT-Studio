How to play

Ivory moves first. Men move one diagonal square forward. Captures are mandatory: jump an adjacent enemy onto an empty square beyond it. When several capture routes exist, choose any route; you must complete it.

Continue a multiple jump with the same piece. Reaching the far row crowns a king and ends that turn. Kings move and capture one diagonal step in either direction. Men capture forward only, following American checkers rules.

Win by leaving your opponent with no legal move. This edition automatically draws on three identical positions or 80 consecutive king moves without a capture or man move. Undo during a jump cancels the unfinished selection; after a turn it restores the board.

Choose two players or a computer opponent. Gentle searches three plies; Deeper searches five. Hint is a suggestion. Keep a portable save to resume; all moves are replayed and checked before a save loads.
